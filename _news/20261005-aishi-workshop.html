Organized as part of the Artificial Intelligence for Spectral/Hyperspectral Imaging (AISHI) initiative, this two-day workshop focuses on using machine learning and artificial intelligence to reconstruct, process, and analyse hyperspectral data. Its scope spans applications in astronomy, geology, environmental monitoring, agriculture, and biomedical imaging.
