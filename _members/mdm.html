Biography

Mauro Dalla Mura is an Associate Professor at Grenoble INP - Université Grenoble Alpes and a researcher at GIPSA-Lab. He is the scientific reference of the Multi-camera Imaging Research and Acquisition (MIRA) Platform and leads the PICSA research team at GIPSA-Lab.

His research activities lie in signal and image processing, with particular interests in remote sensing, hyperspectral and multispectral imaging, multimodal data analysis, image fusion, and computational imaging.

Research Interests
  • Hyperspectral and multispectral imaging
  • Remote sensing
  • Multimodal image analysis and fusion
  • Signal and image processing
  • Machine learning for imaging
  • Computational imaging
