Biography

Mohamad Jouni is a Research Engineer in image processing at the Multi-camera Imaging Research and Acquisition (MIRA) Platform at GIPSA-Lab, Université Grenoble Alpes. His research focuses on multimodal and spectral imaging, including hyperspectral imaging, image registration, image fusion, super-resolution, tensor decomposition, multi-linear algebra, and multi-camera acquisition and processing systems.

His activities combine research, software development, experimental integration, and the supervision of student projects in image and signal processing.

Research Interests
  • Hyperspectral and multispectral imaging
  • Multimodal image fusion
  • Image registration and motion estimation
  • Hyperspectral super-resolution
  • Computational imaging
  • Multi-linear algebra / tensor decomposition
  • Multi-camera acquisition and processing
